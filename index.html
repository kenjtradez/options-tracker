Spot options levels tracker for XAU/USD, EUR/USD and NAS100 with live candlestick chart and manually entered call wall, put wall, max pain and net GEX levels

Spot · Options Levels Tracker
—
Live spot price from Twelve Data. Call wall / put wall / max pain / net GEX are entered manually below — this build has no connected options-data feed, so treat them as your own inputs, not live derived values.
What "Bullish/Bearish lean" means: a simple 3-point check against your entered/computed levels, not a signal or prediction — (1) spot vs gamma flip: above = bullish point; (2) spot vs max pain: above = bullish point; (3) put/call ratio: under 1.0 = bullish point. The badge just counts how many of the 3 currently lean bullish.
